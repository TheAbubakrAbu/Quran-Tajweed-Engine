## Quran text & readings

- **Quranic Arabic text and all qiraat / riwayaat data** — **quran-data-kfgqpc (KFGQPC)** — <https://github.com/thetruetruth/quran-data-kfgqpc>. The default text is the **Hafs an Asim** Uthmani script; alternate readings included: Warsh, Qaloon, ad-Duri, as-Susi, al-Bazzi, Qunbul, Shubah.

> The Quran is the sacred scripture of Islam. The Arabic text must be preserved **exactly** and never altered. Treat it with the respect it is due.

## Corpora beyond the text

These are the corpora this engine ships alongside the Quran itself. They were assembled by
other people, and several were already here before this section existed; naming them is
overdue.

- **Word-by-word glosses** (`word-by-word.json`) - the idea and the assembled corpus come from
  **Tilawa**, by **Jamil Hammoudeh**, used with permission - <https://github.com/jamilhammoudeh/quran-app>.
  The per-word glosses ultimately derive from the **Quranic Arabic Corpus** (Kais Dukes) via the
  **Quran.com (Quran Foundation)** content API - <https://corpus.quran.com/> · <https://api-docs.quran.foundation/>
- **Similar ayahs** (`similar-ayahs.json`) - verified matches from **qurani.ai**'s similar-ayah
  corpus; further phrase-overlap matches generated by **Tilawa**'s matcher; spans and extra pairs
  from the **Quranic Universal Library** - <https://qurani.ai/> · <https://qul.tarteel.ai>
- **Themes** (`themes.json`) - the **Quran Semantic Annotation Corpus (QSAC)** by Ahmad Bilal
  (CC BY 4.0) - <https://github.com/dev-ahmadbilal/quran-semantic-annotation-corpus>
- **Surah sections** (`surah-sections.json`) - **Quranpedia** - <https://quranpedia.net/>
- **The tajweed course** (`tajweed-lessons.json`) - curriculum written by **Jamil Hammoudeh** for
  **Tilawa**, used with permission
- **Morphology** (`morphology.json`) - the **Quranic Arabic Corpus** morphology (Kais Dukes), as
  redistributed by the **Quranic Universal Library** - <https://corpus.quran.com/> · <https://qul.tarteel.ai>
- **Mutashabihat** (`mutashabihat.json`) - the **Quranic Universal Library**'s *Mutashabihat ul
  Quran* phrase set - <https://qul.tarteel.ai>
- **Topics, passage themes and the hizb/ruku/manzil tables** (`quran-topics.json`,
  `ayah-themes.json`, `quran-metadata.json`) - the **Quranic Universal Library**, drawing on
  *The Clear Quran*'s thematic index (Dr. Mustafa Khattab) and the Quranic Arabic Corpus
  ontology - <https://qul.tarteel.ai>
- **Qiraat variants** (`qiraat-variants.json`) - the qiraat matrix published on
  **Quran.com (Quran Foundation)** - <https://quran.com/>. Not a documented API: clear the English
  renderings and explanations with the Quran Foundation before a release that exposes them.
- **Paired variant recordings** (`qiraat-variant-audio.json`) - the reciter/timing table is
  **Tilawa**'s, used with permission; the audio itself is streamed from **EveryAyah**
  (<https://everyayah.com/>) and **MP3 Quran** (<https://mp3quran.net/eng>) and is not bundled.
- **Word of the day** (`word-of-day.json`) - the curation, transliterations and glosses are
  **Tilawa**'s, used with permission; the occurrences are derived from this engine's own text.
- **The 99 Names in depth** (`names-depth.json`) - the roots, themes, explanations and living
  lines are **Tilawa**'s, by **Jamil Hammoudeh**, used with permission; the occurrences are
  derived against this engine's own text. The Names themselves are `names-of-allah.json`, above.
- **Chains of transmission** (`isnad.json`) - compiled from the classical record: **Ibn
  al-Jazari**'s *al-Nashr fi al-Qiraat al-Ashr* and *Ghayat al-Nihayah*, **al-Dani**'s
  *al-Taysir*, and the turuq of *al-Shatibiyyah* and *al-Durrah*, assembled for the **Al-Islam**
  app and kept consistent with the death years this engine already ships.
- **Scientific miracles** (`miracles.json`) - the 202 articles are
  **miracles-of-quran.com**'s, whose author waived rights on the site's own prose -
  <https://www.miracles-of-quran.com/>. The quotations inside them are third-party excerpts, kept
  short and each carrying its own `sourceLabel` and `sourceUrl` (about half are Wikipedia, the
  rest journals and science press); they are quoted under fair use and remain their authors'.
  The corpus came to this engine through the **Al-Islam** app, which took it from **Tilawa**, by
  **Jamil Hammoudeh**, with his permission.
  **The illustrations are deliberately not published here.** They stream from Tilawa's own server
  under permission granted for the app rather than for a public library, so the importer drops
  every `image` block and never publishes `imageBase`; `imagesIncluded` is `false` to say so in
  the data itself. Ask before adding them.
